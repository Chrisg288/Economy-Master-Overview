# Office Supplies / Low-Cost General Merchandise Source Notes

Staples:
- Good for office supplies, furniture, ink/toner, tech, school supplies and business procurement.
- Structured access is more likely through business/eProcurement, PunchOut, EDI or account catalog paths than through a public product API.

Dollarama:
- Good for low-cost general merchandise, seasonal, household basics, party/craft and small office items.
- No obvious public official product API was found.
- Local marketplace listings through Instacart/Uber Eats can provide current local offer snapshots, not a universal Dollarama product master.

General product identity:
- GS1, GS1 Canada ECCnet, UPCitemdb, Go-UPC, Open Product Facts and Open Food Facts can provide barcode/product identity evidence.
- UNSPSC is useful for procurement classification, especially office supplies and business goods.
