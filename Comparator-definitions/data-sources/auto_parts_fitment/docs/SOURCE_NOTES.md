# Auto Parts / Fitment Source Notes

Core doctrine:

- ACES = where the part fits.
- PIES = what the part is.
- Retailer catalog = who sells it, where, when, at what price.
- Safety/evidence record = recalls, warranty, emissions, compliance and evidence.

Auto parts are harder than normal products because fitment matters. A brake pad, filter, bearing or sensor is not fully comparable until the vehicle application and position are known.

Recommended serious path:
1. Use NHTSA vPIC for open vehicle reference experiments.
2. Use Auto Care ACES/PIES standards as the design backbone.
3. Use RockAuto/NAPA public catalogs for examples and links.
4. Use ShowMeTheParts/PDM/Parts Square/Vertical Development if serious commercial feed access is needed.
5. Keep part identity, vehicle fitment, retailer offer and evidence rows separate.
