# Licence and Attribution Notes

Always retain source identifiers, original URLs and retrieval dates.

- Government of Canada datasets generally use the Open Government Licence – Canada; confirm the licence on each dataset page.
- USDA FoodData Central data are U.S. public-domain data and are made available under CC0 terms.
- Open Food Facts is licensed under ODbL and requires attribution/share-alike handling for database reuse.
- ENERGY STAR, NHTSA, CPSC, EPA and openFDA are U.S. government sources, but review each source's metadata and branding/usage terms.
- Health-product records are reference/regulatory data and must not be represented as medical advice.

This package does not relicense third-party source data.
