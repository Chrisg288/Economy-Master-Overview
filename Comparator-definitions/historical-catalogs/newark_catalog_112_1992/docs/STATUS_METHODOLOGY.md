# Historical Catalog Status Methodology

Two statuses are separated.

## Catalog Record Status

For this 1992 edition: **Obsolete**. The listing and catalog price are historical.

## Product Lifecycle Status

Initial value: **Unverified**. An underlying part may still be current, discontinued, available as old stock, or replaced. Do not infer the product lifecycle solely from the catalog age.

Recommended later fields: verified_date, verification_source, current_status, replacement_part_number, alternate_sources, evidence_url, verification_confidence.
