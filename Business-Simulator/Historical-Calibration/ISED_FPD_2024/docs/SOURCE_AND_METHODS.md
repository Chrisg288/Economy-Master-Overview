# Source and Methods

Official source: ISED / Statistics Canada Financial Performance Data 2024. The target population includes businesses earning $30,000–$5 million and $5 million–$20 million. Data are administrative T1/T2 tax records published as industry/geography aggregates.

Sources:
- https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/financial-performance-data/en
- https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/financial-performance-data/en/glossary
