# English Corpus Provenance

## Main linked corpus

- Catalog products: **13,197**
- Historical education services: **5,812**
- Total linked records: **19,009**
- Source language: English

Catalog records were extracted from the uploaded industrial, electronics, laboratory, vehicle-electrical, hardware, hose, modular-framing, fastener, door-hardware, tool and property catalogs. They are factual catalog records, but historical/current status and price still require verification.

Education records came from the uploaded 1995 Independent Study Catalog. They are actual historical service listings, not claims of current course availability.

## QA queue

**4,447** lower-confidence raw catalog candidates remain in `data/review/catalog_candidates_low_confidence.json` for manual or improved automated review. They are retained so data is not silently lost, but they are not mixed into the default comparison tree.
